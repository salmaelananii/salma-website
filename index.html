    Case study · Tactful AI
    CX Leaders Forum
    
    
    
    Tactful AI · Product marketing · Mar 2026 – now
    
    A webinar engine that hands Sales real leads
    
    
    Tactful sells AI customer-experience automation to e-commerce brands in Egypt and the Arab world. The buyers are CX managers, operations managers and e-commerce directors. I relaunched the company's Arabic community as CX Leaders Forum and ran it in two-week sprints, each with targets for registrations, attendance and ICP-fit leads.
    
  
    

  
      
    
    1. Q2 · Mar – Jun 2026
      Relaunch the community
      Tagrobet El 3ameel became CX Leaders Forum, with a new identity, a content calendar of five posts a week and a six-pillar growth plan.
      2. 
    
    2. Q3 · Jul – Sep 2026
      Build the webinar engine
      Launched the series on July 8 and ran 9 webinars in two-week sprints, from TownTeam and Decathlon to RugMorph.
      3. 
    
    3. Q4 · Oct 2026 – now
      Connect it to Sales
      Lead handoff within 48 hours, nurture emails and every recording repurposed into articles, newsletters and clips.
      4. 
  
    

  
    
    
    The problem
    Content brought in a small share of Tactful's leads, and the old community (Tagrobet El 3ameel) had reach without a path to Sales. A webinar counted as a success if people registered, even when none of them could buy.
    
    
    My role
    • Owned the CXLF program end to end: topics, speakers, promotion, registration, follow-up
    • Wrote the 90-day lead-gen plan and every sprint plan and review
    • Ran post-webinar emails and turned each recording into an article, newsletter item and clips
    • Reported results to Sales and leadership each sprint

    What the sprints taught me
    Decisions the data changed
    
  
    
    
    Fit > reach
    The WhatsApp pricing session drew 72 registrations and zero ICP-fit leads. A smaller session on proactive CX drew 58 and produced 9. I started choosing topics by who would show up, not how many.
    
    
    33–54%
    Open rates from warm audiences, against 18–25% from cold lists. Invitations moved to segmented lists (invited vs going) instead of one blast.
    
    
    2× CTR
    Plain-text headlines beat designed ones, and bullet-formatted newsletters doubled click-through. Sends moved to the best windows, around 10:00 and 21:30.
    
    
    1:1 checkpoint
    A "book a free consultation" step inside the Luma registration form raised 1:1 bookings, so registration stopped being the only conversion.
    
    
    Two follow-ups
    Attendees get a Free Consultation email within 24 hours; non-attendees get the recording with the same CTA. Sales gets the MQL list within 48 hours.
    
    
    1 → 4 assets
    Every recording becomes an article, a newsletter item, a carousel and short clips, so one hour of a speaker feeds two weeks of content.

    The documents
    
  
    
    
    GTM plan90-day lead generation plan

    Operating systemSprint plans & reviews
    Two-week sprints, each with targets, a review of target vs actual, and the next sprint's changes.
    
    
    AnalysisEmail & newsletter performance study
    Warm vs cold opens, headline formats and send windows, turned into copy rules for every invite.

    StrategyCXLF six-pillar growth plan
    Brand relaunch, e-membership cards, CX Stories newsletter, webinars, a mini course and a retreat, toward 1,000 members.

        SahlPay · Fintech · 2023
        
    Everything is easy with Sahl
    
        
    مع سهل كله سهل
    
        
    Role: content creator, campaign copyFacebook · Instagram · TikTok
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Brief
    Get people in Egypt to charge prepaid electricity and water cards and pay bills from the Sahl app instead of going to a kiosk.
    
      
    Insight 
    Nobody hates paying a bill. They hate the trip: leaving the house in the heat, the queue, the card that runs out at the worst moment.
    
      
    Idea
    Turn every errand into a tap from the couch. One line carries it: مع سهل كله سهل, and every post shows a moment where the errand simply doesn't happen.
    
    
    
    
    لو شحن كارت الكهرباء خلص فجأه، متقعدش ثانية في الحر!
    
    
    Execution
    • Key visual and campaign line, then sub-campaigns: صيفك سهل for summer and سهل جوه كل بيت for the whole family
    • Social posts for each service: electricity, water, landline, mobile credit, gaming vouchers, beIN, university fees
    • TikTok series and short reels written around everyday moments
    • Push notifications, SMS and in-app microcopy in the same voice
    
    
    
      
      
      
      
    
    
    
    5M+reach for the campaign
    2Morganic TikTok reach
    +500%engagement

        TRU · Fintech · Aug 2025
        
    The most flexible installments in Egypt
    
        
    التقسيط الأكثر مرونة في مصر
    
        
    TVC + digital campaignFacebook · Instagram
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Brief
    Launch TRU's flexible installments: after you buy, you can lower the monthly amount, shorten the duration or pay it off early, all from the app.
    
      
    Insight 
    An installment plan feels like a contract you sign once and live with. Life changes after the purchase, and the plan never does.
    
      
    Idea
    Show flexibility literally. Our hero bends into impossible poses at home, a headstand, a leg on the counter, while he adjusts his installment on the phone.
    
    
    
    
    قسط تقلله، تتجزه، تخلصه في أي وقت بعد ما تشتري!
    
    
    Execution
    • A TVC built on the flexible-poses idea
    • Three key visuals, one per feature: change the amount, shorten the duration, settle any time
    • Two product reels showing TRU Ctrl and TRU limit inside the app

        Telda · Fintech · Ramadan
        
    Telda Comics
    
        
    يوم مش عادي
    
        
    Role: social media executive, writerInstagram Stories
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Brief
    Keep Telda's young audience opening Instagram Stories through Ramadan, when every brand is fighting for the same screen.
    
      
    Insight 
    In Ramadan people come back to a story every day: the series on TV, the same family table. A brand can earn that habit if it has a story worth coming back to.
    
      
    Idea
    A serialized comic told one Instagram Story at a time, with original characters and polls where readers choose what happens next.
    
    
    
    
    Execution
    • Four weekly issues, 18 to 56 frames each
    • Each tap is the next frame; polls decide the plot
    • Characters and dialogue in Egyptian Arabic, written in Telda's voice
    
    
    +100%Instagram Stories engagement in Ramadan
